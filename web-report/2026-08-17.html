The board/code divergence broke, decisively, in the right direction. The shipped API unblocked the board within hours. POST /payments/transfer merged at 06:34 UTC; BVA-I188 “P2P Transfer” came off BLOCKED at 12:59 and into In progress at 13:00. The sequence is consistent with the missing backend having been the actual blocker — the answer the 14 Aug edition asked for and could not get.

The wallets controller still exposes the same seven routes it had on 22 July, and this cycle's +3 API growth is entirely payments. Either the endpoint is on an unmerged branch or the item's state is ahead of its work — and until it resolves, BVA-I170 has no endpoint to wire.
